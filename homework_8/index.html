Illusi

First built from React-Native and JavaScript in a monorepo in 2022, Illusi has always been a Universal Music App. The app idea stemmed from the desire to improve Musi. They lacked many features I wanted like downloading music and SoundCloud support and exporting playlists back to YouTube. Most importantly archiving music; many good small artists and their music gets removed from streaming occasionally and being able to archive them was a very important feature.

Some other features include: Playlist Conversion, Discord Integration, Backpack Unavailable Tracks, Track Statistics, Custom Shuffler, Lyrics Support & Caching, Equalizer, Export Raw Audio, and Support for: YouTube, YouTube Music, Spotify, SoundCloud, Apple Music, Amazon Music, Illusi, Musi, BandLab.
